# Context loading and just-in-time discipline

<a id="CL-001"></a>
**CL-001 — Explicit context-topic adoption.**

Apply to selecting, loading and refreshing work context only when the owning
project explicitly adopts this topic. Installation, a package update or a
reference does not activate it. Preserve the project's accepted entrypoints,
required boot order, client instruction hierarchy and legitimate contributor
paths, including work without an owner-admitted Task. This guidance works with
GitHub, other trackers and local/manual workflows; it grants no execution,
review, publication, merge, release or adoption permission.

<a id="CL-002"></a>
**CL-002 — Retained topic ownership.**

Existing semantic owners retain their procedures and applicability:
[task-handoff](task-handoff.md) for complete relevant Task chronology and current
authority; [self-adoption](self-adoption.md) for independently accepted source
and candidate separation; [independent-review](independent-review.md) for
complete result and primary evidence; [requirement-authority](requirement-authority.md)
for binding requirements; and [proportional-controls](proportional-controls.md)
for control cost and existing proof. These references adopt no additional topic.
Where a topic is not adopted, retain the project's corresponding local rules.

## Start with decision-relevant authority

<a id="CL-003"></a>
**CL-003 — Decision-relevant startup.**

Begin with the project's applicable accepted root/boot instructions, canonical
work authority and current result or change/PR when present. Obtain the exact
Git, check or runtime facts needed for the next decision. Follow the project's
own authority and tracker bindings; this is no new universal startup procedure.
Do not eagerly load broad repository history, status snapshots, unrelated
component manuals or old work artifacts merely because they exist or might
become relevant.

## Load canonical detail when triggered

<a id="CL-004"></a>
**CL-004 — Triggered canonical detail.**

Follow stable routing pointers when a surface becomes material to the task or
decision. Read relevant architecture before changing orchestration or a trust
boundary, the affected component/API contract before changing its interface,
environment/build/qualification guidance when validation depends on it, and
deployment, platform, security, delegation or incident guidance when those
surfaces are active. Use the canonical owning source, including its relevant
qualifiers, exceptions and supported paths.

<a id="CL-005"></a>
**CL-005 — Durable routing and hierarchy.**

Prefer a precise section, path, pinned revision, diff or supported native fact
when sufficient. Keep stable global instructions focused on durable cross-task
invariants and routing; detailed mechanics, platform procedures and fast-changing
facts belong in their canonical owning documents. A lower-level instruction may
specialize a permitted extension point but cannot weaken applicable owner/Task
authority, trust/security, result/review/publication or protected boundaries.
Resolve a genuine conflict through existing project authority before acting;
do not invent a new precedence engine.

## Preserve required completeness

<a id="CL-006"></a>
**CL-006 — Required completeness and material gaps.**

Small initial context and targeted reads do not waive applicable adopted
protocols, required full source or Task chronology, semantic coverage, exact-head
primary evidence, security boundaries or substantive independent assessment.
When a complete document, substantive diff, affected source or paginated surface
is materially needed, read it completely through supported tools. Bound that
reading to the decision and its material dependencies; completeness does not
require unrelated whole-repository discovery. Disclose unavailable or truncated
material and its effect instead of claiming coverage. Reconcile a material gap
before a dependent decision or mutation.

## Refresh what could have changed

<a id="CL-007"></a>
**CL-007 — Targeted mutable refresh.**

After a relevant mutation, interruption/resumption, compaction, recovery or
handoff, refresh live mutable decisions, branch/result/head/check state and
affected environment or validation evidence that could have changed. Preserve
an independently frozen accepted-source identity and useful already-verified
immutable material; do not silently repin or restart broad discovery without a
reason. Reconcile uncertain prior activity or freshness before another mutation.

<a id="CL-008"></a>
**CL-008 — Navigation evidence and proportionality.**

Memory, summaries, caches and generated projections can aid navigation or supply
bounded evidence; they do not by themselves prove current authority, freshness
or acceptance. Verify the affected canonical source when the decision depends
on it. Minimize needless repeated reading and ceremony, preserving correctness.
No source cache, parser, context ledger/service, token/file/tool-call quota,
mandatory bootstrap command or extra approval gate is required by this topic.
